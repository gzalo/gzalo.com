## Privacy

Gzalo.com is a static personal-content website. It has no user accounts or newsletter. If you write to the email address published on the site, your message and email address are used only to reply to you.

The server delivering this site may process routine technical data, such as IP address, browser, and request time, to operate and protect the service. This information is not used for targeted advertising.

## Cookies and third-party services

The site does not set first-party cookies for browsing or sharing links: the **Share** button uses your browser's sharing feature or copies the link locally.

Some pages may include third-party services. Those services load only when you interact with them and apply their own policies:

- **Disqus:** comments do not load automatically. Choosing **Load comments** authorizes Disqus to load; it may use cookies and process information under its [privacy policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).
- **YouTube:** embedded videos are provided by YouTube and may process information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may affect comments or embedded videos.

## External links

This site links to third-party websites. Gzalo.com does not control their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies before providing personal information.
